- Discover: new Back and Discover home buttons, plus a clickable path (Network > Device > Category > Point).
- The mouse Back button and Alt+Left go back too.
- IP addresses, MS/TP MACs, network numbers and device IDs are highlighted again in the packet list, packet details, tables and the Devices tree.
- Fixed: text could briefly draw on top of itself when a page updated.
- Fixed: on smaller screens the packet list's Info column was pushed off screen, and some pages were cut off on the right.
- Fixed: in light mode the unselected Capture / Discover switch was hard to read; checkboxes after a theme change.
- MS/TP trunk: clearer message when a capture has no token passing in it.
- PuTTY is now included: Tools > PuTTY opens it for SSH, Telnet or a serial console.
- Discover over MS/TP: choose Connection: MS/TP, pick your USB RS-485 adapter, press Join trunk. The PC listens first, picks a free MAC address, joins the token ring and can scan and read points directly on the trunk (no router needed).
- Excel export: every Save / Export menu now offers an Excel (.xlsx) version next to CSV.
- Discover: Active alarms button on a device lists points in alarm (state, acknowledged, type).
- Point details: new Alarm & status section (status flags, event state, high/low limits, deadband, notification class) and a Read all properties button with Copy and Export.
- While Discover is joined to an MS/TP trunk, its frames show in the Live capture tab so you can see the traffic.
- Sorting a very large capture no longer freezes the window, and re-sorting is much faster.
- Smaller download: unused parts of Qt are no longer included.
- PuTTY button next to Capture / Discover at the top opens PuTTY in its own window.
