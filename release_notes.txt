- Packet details now show the full contents of every BACnet message, like Wireshark: invoke ID, max response size, the service and every parameter – objects, properties, values with units and states, priorities, time stamps, errors – nested as deep as the message goes. COV notifications show their changed values in the Info column too.
- Click any highlighted IP address, MS/TP MAC, network or device ID (in Findings, Devices, Compare and the other tables) to see just the packets that involve it. New display filters: ip == "10.0.0.5" and mac == 5.
- Hover over a line in the packet details that is cut off to read all of it.
